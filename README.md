Overview

This report gives a single-page view of customer feedback. It shows how customers score the business on NPS, how they rate their overall experience, and which channels and visit types they come through. A date range filter lets you track these measures over time.

Dashboard contents
Visual	Type	What it shows
NPS Score	Gauge	Overall Net Promoter Score
NPS Category	Clustered bar	Responses grouped as promoters, passives and detractors
Overall Experience	Card and bar	Total and average overall experience rating
Feedback	Clustered column	Rating score by feedback category and feedback item
Purpose of Visit	Donut	Share of responses by reason for visiting
Gender	Donut	Response split by gender
Source of Information	Clustered bar	Channels customers used to give feedback
Source Trend	Ribbon chart	How feedback sources change by year, quarter, month and day

Filters (slicers): Purpose, Gender and Checkout Date.
